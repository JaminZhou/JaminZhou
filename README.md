## Featured projects

### CalcBird

CalcBird is a focused iPhone and iPad shopping helper for calculating and comparing real unit prices when package sizes, currencies, coupons, deposits, and full-reduction discounts make the shelf price hard to trust. The iPad app can also run in Designed for iPad mode on compatible Apple silicon Macs.

- [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762044720)

### Rouse

Rouse keeps your Mac awake with a fast menu bar toggle, a calm dashboard for timed sessions, display sleep, and battery-aware controls, plus an official CLI for Terminal, scripts, and local automation.

- [Download on the Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rouse-stay-awake/id6760773101?mt=12)

### Hushtrail

Hushtrail 0.1.0 clears selected app history and helps review possible leftovers from apps you no longer use. It remembers chosen apps, explains items it keeps, and moves selected leftovers to macOS Trash. English and Simplified Chinese; requires macOS 27.0 or later. Finder capabilities currently require macOS 27.0.0.

- [Download for Mac and read release notes](https://github.com/JaminZhou/hushtrail-releases/releases/tag/v0.1.0)
- [Visit the product page](https://jaminzhou.com/hushtrail/)
